GPP 100 − R 50 heat = NPP 50
GPP: energy fixed in photosynthesis. R: used in the producers' own respiration, lost as heat. NPP: stored as producer biomass (algae, phytoplankton, water plants) — food for grazers and decomposers.
NPP 50 → TL2 10 eaten + Detritus 40
Grazers can only eat what the producers keep after respiration — the NPP, not the GPP. About 10 of it reaches TL2. The other 40 — dead and uneaten plant and algal matter — sinks as detritus to the decomposers (step 6).
Humus → NH4+ · NO3− · PO43− → TL1 uptake
Mineralisation: microbes (bacteria) break down humus further… …releasing inorganic nutrients such as ammonium, nitrate and phosphate. Producers take them up. Matter cycles; energy does not.
